# Assessment rubric

| Criterion | Excellent | Satisfactory | Needs development |
|---|---|---|---|
| Synthetic-situation documentation | Detailed and reflexive account of model, interface, prompts, categories, participation, context, unknowns, and limits | Covers main system conditions and some limits | Treats images as isolated outputs or omits key contextual conditions |
| Theoretical orientation | Uses sensitising concepts clearly and distinguishes them from code labels | Uses relevant theory with partial distinction between concepts and features | Uses theory as a list of assumptions or leaves framework unclear |
| Feature operationalisation | Definitions are observable, precise, and include inclusion, exclusion, and borderline guidance | Most features are clear; some boundaries require refinement | Definitions depend on inference, overlap heavily, or lack coding guidance |
| Collaborative reasoning | Documents disagreement, negotiation, and revision productively | Shows some group discussion and revision | Presents codebook as fixed or does not document decisions |
| Pilot and refinement | Uses pilot findings to make specific, justified revisions | Conducts pilot and makes some revisions | Pilot is absent or does not influence codebook |
| Descriptive analysis | Uses counts or patterns cautiously and connects them to image review | Provides basic descriptive evidence | Makes claims without evidence or overgeneralises from small samples |
| Interpretation and limits | Integrates theory, visual evidence, context, alternatives, and limits | Connects theory and findings with some caution | Overstates causal, demographic, or model-wide claims |
| Ethics and reflexivity | Addresses representational stakes, uncertainty, data conditions, and possible harm | Notes major ethical issues | Omits ethical and political implications |
