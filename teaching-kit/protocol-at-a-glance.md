
# Protocol at a glance

```text
1. Synthetic situation
   Document model, interface, prompts, participants, categories, settings, and unknowns.
                ↓
2. Theoretical orientation
   Identify concepts that guide attention toward representational power.
                ↓
3. Corpus inspection
   Record recurring visible patterns before forming fixed categories.
                ↓
4. Seed features
   Develop provisional features with observable visual bases.
                ↓
5. Collaborative codebook
   Define, compare, merge, split, revise, retain, or remove features.
                ↓
6. Pilot and revision
   Test on a new sample; document ambiguity and revise definitions.
                ↓
7. Descriptive analysis
   Examine occurrence, accumulation, co-occurrence, and contextualised metadata patterns.
                ↓
8. Situated interpretation
   Relate visual patterns to theory and the full image-production arrangement.
```
