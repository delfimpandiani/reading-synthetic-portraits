### Instructions

- Use short, descriptive feature names.
- Define what can be seen, not what the depicted subject intends or feels.
- Add inclusion and exclusion criteria before pilot coding.
- Use `strong`, `weak`, or another clearly defined feature type.
- Record theoretical connections as interpretive resources, not as feature definitions.
- Mark features as `provisional`, `active`, `revised`, or `removed`.
