
# ctrl.alt.img: a situated application of the protocol

This folder documents how the reusable protocol for theory-informed, inductive feature coding was applied to **ctrl.alt.img**, a participatory AI photo-booth installation developed by affect lab.

The materials show how a feature codebook can emerge from a specific synthetic situation. Visitors encountered an enclosed photo booth, a facial classifier, editable demographic predictions, free-text self-description, a fixed prompt template, photo-booth framing, exhibition settings, and a legacy diffusion-based image-generation system. The resulting portraits were generated through this full sociotechnical arrangement.

## What this folder contains

- a detailed account of the ctrl.alt.img synthetic situation;
- a stage-by-stage account of the application of the protocol;
- the taxonomy diagram and explanation of its levels;
- the ctrl.alt.img codebook in PDF and CSV form;
- a cleaned public-facing codebook format;
- focal-feature definitions and descriptive results;
- aggregate quantitative outputs;
- data-access and ethics guidance.

## How to use these materials

The ctrl.alt.img taxonomy and codebook provide a worked example of the general protocol. They can support comparison, adaptation, extension, correction, and critique. They should be used with attention to the corpus’s specific conditions of production.

The materials do not provide a universal taxonomy of representational harm, a general evaluation of diffusion models, or causal evidence about individual prompt fields, demographic categories, or training data.
